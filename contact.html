FAQ

Quick answers

Where is Magic Touch Retreats?

Magic Touch Retreats is at Mbezi Beach, Dar es Salaam. Use the Google Maps, Uber or Bolt links on this page to find the exact doorstep.

What are your opening hours?

We are open every day from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

How do I book a treatment?

Tap Book on WhatsApp, or pick services on the Services page and continue on WhatsApp. Reception replies and confirms your time.

How much do treatments cost?

Prices start from TZS 10,000 for nails, 15,000 for waxing, 20,000 for massages and 30,000 for skin care. Braids and hair are priced on request. The full list is on the Services page.

Can I order products online?

Yes. Add products to your list on the Shop page and send the order on WhatsApp. Our team confirms stock, price and delivery with you.

Do you offer a free skin consultation?

Yes, a free skin consultation is part of our offers. See the Offers page for the current packages.

Do you teach beauty courses?

Yes. The Magic Touch Beauty Academy runs courses in skincare, nails, makeup and hair styling. Send an enquiry on the Academy page.
